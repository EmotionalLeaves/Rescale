Rescale makes small, compressed or old images bigger and sharper using Real-ESRGAN models that run entirely on your phone.

• Photo and Anime/Art modes, 2× and 4×
• Uses your GPU (Vulkan) when it can, with automatic CPU fallback
• Handles very large outputs by streaming them to disk
• Works in the background with a progress notification
• Before/after comparison with zoom
• Share any image to "Upscale with Rescale"

No internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no watermarks. Open source (GPL-3.0).

Upscaling invents plausible detail. It doesn't recover what the original never captured.
